#### school cafeteria

Recently, it has become common for school cafeterias to have long queues during lunch time. This situation occurs because the number of person eating at the same time is large while the capacity is limited, and the serving speed isn't sufficient to meet the demand.

To solve this problem, schools can take several measures. First, opening more windows in each cafeteria can increase parallel efficiency and reduce waiting time. In addition, building more cafeterias or restaurants in different locations can help students at different teaching buildings enjoy the same convenience——having lunch near their dormitories. School can also hire more skilled or faster staff, or adopt a self service or assembly-line model, which can further speed up the progress.

Another effective approach is to manage the flow of students. For instance, school can arrange different ending time of morning classes, so the students head to lunch at staggered times. This can effectively reduce overcrowding and make the dining experience smoother.

Through these ways, I believe that we will have a quicker, organized, and more efficient cafeteria in the near future.

#### Overfishing

In recent years, overfishing has become common. This practice may not only diminishes the living space of fish, but also exacerbates environmental issues such as global warming and sea-level rise. Further more, it disrupts the biological chain and accelerates the extinction of endangered species.

Several factors contribute to this situation. On the consumer side, continuous demand for shark fin, fish oil and culinary preference drives the expansion of the fishing industry. On the production side, the usage of trawling nets that even captures small fish undermines the sustainability of marine ecosystems.

To address these problems, a number of measures can be taken. First, government can implements regulations requiring trawlers with larger meshes and seasonal fishing bans, enabling the fish population to recover and help restore ecological balance. In addition, the  propaganda of the idea of marine conservation and sustainability development may reduce the consumption of fish-made commodities.

Through these ways, we will have a cleaner sea that develops sustainably and freely.
